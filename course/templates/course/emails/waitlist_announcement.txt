Our MamiFit classes often fill up quickly, so we now have a waiting list.

How it works:
1. If a class is fully booked, click «Auf die Warteliste» (join waiting list) in the class overview. You need at least 1 credit.
2. As soon as a spot opens up, you'll get an email.
3. Click «Book now» to grab the spot. First come, first serve: the first person to click gets it. Only then is 1 credit deducted.

If someone was faster, you stay on the waiting list and get another email when the next spot opens up. You can leave the waiting list at any time in the class overview.

Can't make it? Please cancel your booking, even if it's less than 24 hours before the class. You won't get your credit back, but another woman on the waiting list can still take your spot. Thank you!
